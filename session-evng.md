   - **RAG/Retrieval**: Answers *What educational information should be taught?* based on source documents.

5. **Fallback Strategy**: If local Text-to-Video is unavailable or slow, falls back to Image/Comic-style scenes + audio narration to keep the learning loop functional.
6. **MVP Focus**: Complete end-to-end loop covering profile selection, topic/material input, RAG, story synthesis, scene narration + visual generation, and final video assembly.
